## What we make

- **Morph Switcher**: automatic keyboard layout switcher for Windows.
- **Dark Optimization Pack**: Windows optimization.
- **Vox Media Player**: a media player.

Release notes, beta access and news live on [abyssc0re.com](https://abyssc0re.com).

## Good to know

- We are not affiliated with Valve or its game Deadlock, and we do not make game cheats.
- Found a problem? Report it on the site or open an issue in the project's repository.
- We are a small team and we keep a friendly tone. Please do the same: see our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/deadlock-ink/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
